🎧 Listening & Reading

Listen to Yusuf's story. Notice the words in teal.

My name is Yusuf. I am from Somaliland. Three years ago, I arrived in the United Kingdom. It was a big change. My English was not very good at first.

In my first month, I attended an English class every day. I met many new people there. I made some good friends. We practised English together every evening.

Last year, I found a job at a supermarket. I worked hard and I learned a lot. My manager helped me very much. I did not miss many days at work.

Yesterday, I went to a job interview. I felt nervous but I spoke well. I answered all the questions. When I got home, I called my mother to tell her. She was very proud of me!

📐 Grammar — Past Simple

Use the past simple for finished actions in the past. Regular verbs: add -ed. Irregular verbs must be memorised.
