🎧 Listening & Reading

Listen to Amina's story. Notice the words in teal.

My name is Amina. I am from Djibouti, and I live in Birmingham now. I love cooking and eating good food. Food is very important to me!

I like fruit and vegetables very much. I like rice, lentils, and fish. I eat a lot of fruit — fruit is an uncountable noun in English when we talk about it in general, but we can say "three pieces of fruit" or "two apples".

I don't like very sweet food. I don't like fizzy drinks either. My doctor says I need to drink water every day — water is uncountable too! But I can say "two glasses of water".

Every day I eat an egg for breakfast. Eggs are countable — I can say one egg, two eggs. I also eat some bread — bread is uncountable. I love my morning routine. Good food makes me feel strong and happy!

📐 Grammar

❤️ Like / Don't Like

SubjectPositiveNegative
I / You / We / Theylike + noun/verb-ingdon't like + noun/verb-ing
He / She / Itlikes + noun/verb-ingdoesn't like + noun/verb-ing
📝 Examples
✓I like fish. / She likes cooking.
✗I don't like sugar. / He doesn't like vegetables.

🔢 Countable & Uncountable Nouns

CountableUncountable
an egg / two eggsrice (not "a rice")
an apple / three appleswater (not "a water")
a banana / five bananasbread (not "a bread")
a lemon / four lemonsfruit (in general)
💡 With uncountable nouns, use: some / a piece of / a glass of / a bowl of
→some rice · a glass of water · a piece of bread · a bowl of soup
