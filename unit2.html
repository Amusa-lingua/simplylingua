🎧 Listening & Reading

Listen to Fatima's story. The words in teal are all present simple verbs — can you see the pattern?

My name is Fatima. I work at a busy market in Hargeisa. Every day I wake up at six o'clock. I make tea and I eat breakfast with my family.

I leave home at seven thirty. I take the bus to the market. The journey takes about twenty minutes. I arrive at the market at eight o'clock.

I sell vegetables and spices. Many customers come to my stall every morning. My colleague Halima helps me in the afternoons. We close the stall at five o'clock.

In the evening, I cook dinner for my family. After dinner, I study English online. I go to bed at ten o'clock. It is a busy life, but I love it!

📐 Grammar

The Present Simple describes regular actions, habits, and facts. We use it for things that happen every day, always, usually, etc.

✅ Positive

SubjectVerb formExample
I / You / We / Theybase formI work at the market.
He / She / It+ s / esShe works in the afternoon.

❌ Negative

SubjectPatternExample
I / You / We / Theydo not (don't) + verbI don't work on Sundays.
He / She / Itdoes not (doesn't) + verbShe doesn't work on Fridays.

❓ Questions

QuestionYesNo
Do you work every day?Yes, I do.No, I don't.
Does she sell vegetables?Yes, she does.No, she doesn't.
📝 Spelling rules for he/she/it
Most verbs:+swork → works, leave → leaves
-ch, -sh, -ss, -x, -o:+esgo → goes, teach → teaches
consonant + y:y→iesstudy → studies, carry → carries
⏰ Time expressions
every day / every morning / every week
at six o'clock · at night · in the morning
always · usually · often · sometimes · never
